# Staging rebuild

The staging site at https://gishistory.pm4gis.nz is being rebuilt as a narrative-first, data-driven NZ GIS history reader. This structural sample has no fixed chapter count and no PDF feature.

The sample dataset contains one story, two passages, three records, two dated events, two relationships and two themes. Its example follows AAM / AAMHatch and NorthSouth GIS. The collection is designed to grow through editable content records rather than chapter-specific page templates.

The site is published from the `main` branch of `pm4gis/nz-gis-history-rebuild` to Cloudflare Pages project `nzgis-history-stage`. The production domain for that project is `gishistory.pm4gis.nz`; `history.pm4gis.nz` is a separate canonical site and is not part of this rebuild.
